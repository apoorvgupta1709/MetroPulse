Privacy Policy

Application: MetroPulse: Metro Route & Map (com.apoorv.metropulse)
Effective Date: November 9, 2026
Developer: Apoorv Gupta

1. Overview

MetroPulse is designed to prioritize commuter privacy and convenience. We believe daily transit tools should never mandate invasive tracking. MetroPulse is fully functional offline and does not require an account or registration for general transit routing.

2. Location Information

When you use the "Metro Near Me" feature, the app requests access to your device's fine and coarse location.

  • Local Processing Only: Your coordinates are processed solely on your local device to compute distances to nearby transit stations.
  • We do not store, log, transmit, or sell your personal location data.

3. Account Information & Authentication on Demand

MetroPulse offers zero-friction access without mandatory login. When an optional purchase (e.g. Prime Commuter Pass) is initiated, secure authentication is invoked to link your entitlement to your account, allowing you to restore your purchase across devices and re-installs.

4. Advertising & Monetization

For free-tier commuters, third-party advertising is served via Google AdMob in accordance with Google Play Developer policies.

  • Google AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage metrics to serve non-personalized or personalized ads as permitted by your OS settings.
  • Users with a Prime Commuter Pass have all advertising permanently disabled.
  • AdMob publisher verification is maintained via the sanctioned app-ads.txt on this domain.

5. Security & Inquiries

We implement strict industry standards and production-locked security rules. For support, data requests, or privacy questions, please contact our developer support team.

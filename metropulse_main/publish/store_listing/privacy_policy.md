# Privacy Policy for MetroPulse

**Effective Date:** November 9, 2026  
**Developer:** Apoorv Gupta (`apoorvgupta1709`)  
**Application:** MetroPulse (`com.apoorv.metropulse`)

---

## 1. Overview
MetroPulse is designed to prioritize commuter privacy and convenience. We believe daily transit tools should never mandate invasive tracking. MetroPulse is functional offline and does not require an account or registration for general transit routing.

## 2. Location Information
- When you use the "Metro Near Me" feature, the app requests access to your device's fine and coarse location.
- **Local Processing Only**: Your coordinates are processed solely on your local device to compute distance to nearby transit stations. We do not store, log, transmit, or sell your personal location data.

## 3. Account Information & Authentication on Demand
- MetroPulse offers zero-friction access without mandatory login.
- When an optional purchase (e.g. Prime Commuter Pass) is initiated, Google Sign-In is invoked to securely link your entitlement to your Google account UID, allowing you to restore your purchase across devices and re-installs.

## 4. Advertising & Monetization
- For free tier commuters, third-party advertising is served via Google AdMob in accordance with Google Play Developer policies.
- AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage metrics to serve non-personalized or personalized ads as permitted by your OS settings.
- Prime Commuter Pass subscribers have all advertising permanently disabled.

## 5. Security & Inquiries
We implement strict industry standards and production-locked security rules. For support or privacy questions, reach out to `apoorvgupta1709` on GitHub.
